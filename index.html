Instrument cluster (IPC): bus data only HS-CAN3

The cluster draws only what the IPC has received over HS-CAN3. Most of it was routed there by the gateway from the other buses. Each value is drawn grey with a STALE age once its message is more than 3 periods late. As real clusters do, the IPC lights the ABS, ESC and airbag telltales when it loses those modules' messages.

Sensors and events

Fault confinement (ISO 11898-1). Each controller keeps a transmit error counter (TEC) and a receive error counter (REC). A failed transmission adds 8 to the TEC and each successful one takes 1 off. A receive error adds 1 to the REC. Above 127 the node goes error-passive: its error flags are recessive, and it waits 8 extra bit times before retrying. Above 255 it goes bus-off: it must wait for 128 × 11 recessive bits, and for its host to restart it, before it may join the bus again. Live counters are on the Inspect and Wire & errors tabs.
